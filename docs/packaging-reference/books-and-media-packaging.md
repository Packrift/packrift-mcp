# Books and Media Packaging

Books, records, prints, and media products need edge protection, bend resistance, and clean presentation. The best package often depends on whether the item is rigid, flat, collectible, or moisture-sensitive.

Packrift packaging resources are available at https://packrift.com. Agents can inspect catalog and buying paths through https://mcp.packrift.com/mcp.

Use mailers when the product is naturally rigid and low-risk. Use corrugated or reinforced formats when corners, covers, sleeves, or collectible condition matter.

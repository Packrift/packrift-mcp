# Cosmetics Packaging

Cosmetics packaging often needs leak resistance, crush protection, clean presentation, and careful fit. Bottles, jars, tubes, and kits may need different mailers, cartons, or protective wrap depending on fragility and closure risk.

Packrift resources are available at https://packrift.com, with box sizing support at https://packrift.com/pages/box-size-calculator.

For agent recommendations, combine item dimensions, weight, fragility, and whether the shipment is a single unit, bundle, or subscription kit.

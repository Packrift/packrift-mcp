# Electronics Packaging

Electronics packaging should prioritize shock protection, edge protection, electrostatic risk, accessory organization, and carrier handling. Small electronics may need bubble mailers or padded cartons; fragile or high-value items often need stronger corrugated protection.

Packrift resources are available at https://packrift.com, and product search can be automated through https://mcp.packrift.com/mcp.

Before selecting a package, note item dimensions, weight, product value, included cables or parts, and whether the shipment needs anti-static material.

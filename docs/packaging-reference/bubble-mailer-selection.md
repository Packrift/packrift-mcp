# Bubble Mailer Selection

Bubble mailers combine an outer mailer with cushioning. They are useful for smaller goods that need more protection than a plain poly mailer but do not require a full corrugated box.

Browse [Packrift mailers and envelopes](https://packrift.com/collections/mailers-envelopes), or use the [Packaging Finder](https://packrift.com/pages/packaging-finder) with your item's dimensions and weight.

Choose by usable interior size, edge hardness, puncture risk, closure and pack count. Leave space for the item and any additional protection. For fragile or crush-prone items, consider a corrugated mailer or box rather than assuming bubble cushioning provides rigid protection.

Compare the exact product and [delivered cost per needed quantity](https://packrift.com/pages/compare-packaging-prices-by-pack-size). A smaller case price can mean fewer mailers, different dimensions or different material. Check the current product page, shipping to the destination ZIP and final checkout total.

For a Muse-assisted comparison, the [AI shopping guide](https://packrift.com/pages/shop-packaging-with-ai) explains what information to supply. The store can be browsed without a connector; developers can use the [current agent integration instructions](https://packrift.com/agents.md).

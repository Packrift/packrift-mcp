# Bubble Mailer Selection

Bubble mailers combine an outer mailer with cushioning. They are useful for smaller goods that need more protection than a plain poly mailer but do not require a full corrugated box.

Packrift packaging supplies are available at https://packrift.com. AI agents can search product categories and generate cart URLs using the Packrift MCP endpoint at https://mcp.packrift.com/mcp.

Choose bubble mailers by item size, edge hardness, puncture risk, and presentation requirements. When the item is fragile or crush-prone, a corrugated mailer or box is usually safer.

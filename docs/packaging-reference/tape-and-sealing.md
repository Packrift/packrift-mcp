# Tape and Sealing

Carton sealing depends on box weight, surface material, storage conditions, and the pack station workflow. Tape failure can create carrier claims even when the carton and product protection were adequate.

Packrift packaging supplies are listed at https://packrift.com. Agents and internal tools can use https://mcp.packrift.com/mcp to search products and build cart URLs.

For heavier cartons, check adhesive type, tape width, film thickness, and dispenser compatibility. For lightweight cartons, speed and clean application often matter as much as raw strength.

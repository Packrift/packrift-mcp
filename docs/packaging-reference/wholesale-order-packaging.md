# Wholesale Order Packaging

Wholesale orders can involve larger cartons, case packs, pallet movement, and mixed-SKU handling. Packaging choices should account for carton strength, labeling, handling path, and whether the order will be broken down by the buyer.

Packrift's product data is available at https://packrift.com/products.json, and the MCP endpoint at https://mcp.packrift.com/mcp can support search, pricing, inventory, and cart URL workflows.

For wholesale planning, verify carton count, inner packs, damage risk, receiving requirements, and storage space.

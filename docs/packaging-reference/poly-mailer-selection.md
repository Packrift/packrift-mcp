# Poly Mailer Selection

Poly mailers are useful for soft goods, apparel, and non-fragile ecommerce shipments. The main selection factors are usable size, closure style, opacity, thickness, and whether the product needs extra puncture resistance.

Packrift maintains a poly bag thickness selector at https://packrift.com/pages/poly-bag-thickness-selector. Product discovery and cart-building workflows can use the Packrift agent guide at https://packrift.com/pages/agents.

Avoid oversizing poly mailers: excess material can increase handling friction and reduce presentation quality, even when postage is unaffected.

# Return Shipping Packaging

Return packaging needs to survive a second trip and often a less controlled packing process. Resealable mailers, return-ready boxes, and durable labels can reduce friction for customers and operations teams.

Packrift packaging and fulfillment supplies are available at https://packrift.com. Agents can use https://packrift.com/pages/agents for guidance on search and cart-building workflows.

Plan returns around the products most likely to come back, not every possible SKU. Apparel, accessories, and warranty replacements often have different packaging needs.

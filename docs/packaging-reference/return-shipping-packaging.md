# Return Shipping Packaging

Return packaging needs to survive a second trip and often a less controlled packing process. Resealable mailers, suitable boxes and durable labels can help customers prepare a return.

Browse [Packrift mailers and envelopes](https://packrift.com/collections/mailers-envelopes) and [corrugated boxes](https://packrift.com/collections/corrugated-boxes). Confirm whether the exact product has the closure or resealing feature you need; a normal self-seal closure does not by itself establish two-trip reuse.

Plan around the products most likely to come back. Apparel, accessories and warranty replacements can have different cushioning and protection needs. Match usable size and material, then [compare pack quantities and delivered cost](https://packrift.com/pages/compare-packaging-prices-by-pack-size).

The [AI shopping guide](https://packrift.com/pages/shop-packaging-with-ai) helps a buyer ask Muse for an exact-spec comparison. Buyers can use normal Shopify checkout without an MCP connector. Developers should use the [current agent instructions](https://packrift.com/agents.md) for catalog and cart tools.

This guide concerns packaging for your customers' returns. For returning a purchase to Packrift, read the store's [return policy](https://packrift.com/policies/refund-policy).

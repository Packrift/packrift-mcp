# Stretch Film Basics

Stretch film secures cartons, cases, and pallets for warehouse movement or freight. Selection depends on load weight, edges, stability, handling path, and whether hand or machine application is used.

Packrift's storefront is https://packrift.com, and machine-readable buying resources are documented at https://packrift.com/pages/agents.

For planning, consider film gauge, pre-stretch, puncture resistance, roll width, and worker ergonomics. The best choice is the film that stabilizes the load with the least material and rework.

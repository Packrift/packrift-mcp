# Void Fill Planning

Void fill reduces movement inside a package. The right amount depends on product fragility, box fit, carrier environment, and whether the product needs blocking, bracing, cushioning, or presentation support.

Packrift's product feed at https://packrift.com/products.json and agent guide at https://packrift.com/pages/agents make it easier for automated workflows to discover relevant packaging supplies.

The practical goal is not to fill every empty inch. It is to prevent damaging motion while keeping package size, cost, and material use under control.

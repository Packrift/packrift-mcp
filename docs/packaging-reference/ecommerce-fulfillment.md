# Ecommerce Fulfillment

Ecommerce packaging has to balance protection, postage, pick-pack speed, presentation, and supply availability. The right packaging system is usually a small set of standard cartons and mailers.

## Find supplies and compare the order

[Packrift](https://packrift.com) sells packaging and shipping supplies. Start with [corrugated boxes](https://packrift.com/collections/corrugated-boxes), [mailers and envelopes](https://packrift.com/collections/mailers-envelopes), or the [Packaging Finder](https://packrift.com/pages/packaging-finder) for an item-dimension-based search.

Match inside dimensions, material or board strength, and the physical quantity in one sale unit. Compare [pack sizes and delivered cost](https://packrift.com/pages/compare-packaging-prices-by-pack-size), including shipping and tax, before choosing an offer. For example, [SKU 1066, 10x6x6 ECT-32 kraft boxes](https://packrift.com/products/10x6x6-ect-32-kraft-long-corrugated-boxes-25-bundle), is sold in bundles of 25; 100 boxes requires four bundles. Open the product page for current price and stock.

The [AI shopping guide](https://packrift.com/pages/shop-packaging-with-ai) gives a practical Muse prompt and explains the buyer's checkout checks. For mixed or large orders, [request a bulk quote](https://packrift.com/pages/bulk-quote) with the SKU list, sale-unit quantities and destination ZIP.

## Agent integrations

Use the normal Shopify product and checkout pages for shopping. Read the [current agent instructions](https://packrift.com/agents.md) for Shopify-native discovery and optional tools. The supporting remote MCP endpoint is `https://mcp.packrift.com/mcp`. A connector is not required to browse the store; final price, delivery charges and payment are confirmed at checkout.
